    noScribe#

    noScribe is an open-source transcription app for interviews, qualitative research, and journalism. Transcription runs locally on your machine and is based on Whisper, faster-whisper, and pyannote.

    Download & Installation GitHub Releases Code & technical docs

    Why noScribe?#

    • Offline-first: No cloud uploads, no account required.
    • Open source: GPL-3.0, transparent and future-proof.
    • Multilingual: 60+ languages.
    • Speaker identification: Associate segments with speakers.
    • Built-in editor: Review and correct transcripts in the app.

    Privacy by design#

    This website uses no cookies, no tracking, and no external requests. noScribe itself is designed as a local desktop app and processes audio offline.

    Who is it for?#

    • Interview transcription in research & teaching
    • Journalism workflows
    • Qualitative studies and analysis
    • Project and field notes
